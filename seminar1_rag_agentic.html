Why "Agentic"?

  • Agent = system that can plan, decide, and act autonomously
  • Unlike standard RAG (fixed steps), agentic RAG decides dynamically how many retrieval steps to take
  • Can decompose complex multi-part queries into sub-questions
  • 2025–2026 is called "The Year of Agents" in AI
