  • Every box with a dashed border is editable. Click it, type, then click away. Nothing is saved to the file until you download.
  • French rows show two boxes. The French one is what gets saved. The blue English one is a working view: edit the English, click away, and the French is updated in place with only the words your edit touched. Edit the French and the English refreshes.
  • Step 1 is keyboard driven. 1 plausible, 0 not. ← → move, u clears. Keys are ignored while you are typing in a box.
  • Step 2 candidates are deliberately unlabelled. They are pooled from the current gold label, the surrounding clauses, every system's retrieved chunks and a keyword sweep, then shuffled. You are not told which is which, so your pick cannot be pulled toward what a system happened to retrieve.
  • Pick one or two chunks, then cut the parts that do not answer the question straight out of them. The pruned text is what becomes the ground truth.
  • Draft gold answer sends your question and your pruned chunks, with their article and clause numbers, and returns a draft you then edit. It cannot see any system's answer or score, so neither can influence you.
